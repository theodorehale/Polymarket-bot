## HO-STR-0002 / HO-STR-0015 — NegRisk and logical baskets
- Official current docs https://docs.polymarket.com/concepts/negative-risk confirm atomic conversion of one NO position to YES positions in other outcomes, and special rules for augmented/Other outcomes. Atomic **conversion** is not atomic multi-leg acquisition or guaranteed fills.
- Official current fees https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees state `fee = shares * feeRate * p * (1-p)`, Crypto taker coefficient 0.07, Combos coefficient 0.05; these are different fee schedules and must be versioned by contract/date. For hypothetical 10 shares each at $0.48 and $0.49, a YES+NO $0.97 gross cost yields $0.30 theoretical gross payout surplus but ~$0.34965 two-leg Crypto taker fees, i.e. -$0.04965 before slippage (SYNTHETIC FORMULA CHECK, not a live order-book opportunity). No new independent real basket fills or resolution.
- Same mechanisms and old blockers as 0002/0015: exact mutually exclusive payoff states, incomplete Other definition, depth, partial fills, latency, conversion eligibility and settlement. Status RESEARCH CANDIDATE, not READY.
